Common fixes

Days aren't recording automatically

Check Settings → IFTA Tracker → Location is set to Always, and that your tax home pin is set (IFTA Tracker Settings → Tax home). Nights with the phone off or in airplane mode become "were you away?" cards instead — IFTA Tracker never invents a day.

A receipt scanned the wrong total

Tap the receipt and correct the amount — the photo stays attached either way. Faded thermal paper in low light is the usual culprit; a second shot in better light usually reads clean.

How do I cancel?

IFTA Tracker Settings → Manage or cancel subscription (takes you straight to Apple's sheet), or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Your records stay exportable after you cancel.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restore the new phone from your iCloud or computer backup and your IFTA Tracker data comes with it. Confirm your tax home pin after restoring.

How do I delete everything?

IFTA Tracker Settings → Your data → Delete all data. It's immediate and irreversible — there's no server copy to linger.
