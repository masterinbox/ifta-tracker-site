Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

This policy is short because our architecture makes most privacy policies unnecessary: IFTA Tracker has no accounts, no login, and no IFTA Tracker server that receives your records. Your ledger lives on your device.

What IFTA Tracker stores, and where

  • Day records, receipts, tax-home location, quiz answers, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. If you enable iCloud sync (when available), they are also stored in your private iCloud database, which is controlled by your Apple ID and is not readable by us.
  • Location: IFTA Tracker uses low-power location events (significant location changes, visit detection, and a single geofence around your tax home) to detect overnight stays away from your tax home. Location history is processed on your device, stored on your device, and coordinates on day records are rounded to roughly city-level precision. Location data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Raw location events are automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Receipt photos are stored on your device (and in your private iCloud if sync is enabled). Text recognition runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework — images are not sent anywhere for processing.

What we receive

  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We receive anonymized transaction status (for example, "an annual subscription is active") — never your name, card, or address.
  • Anonymous usage analytics: we may collect privacy-preserving, aggregate usage events (for example, "a receipt was scanned successfully") to improve the app. These events contain no name, no email, no location, no dollar amounts from your ledger, and no identifiers linked to your identity, and are not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Crash reports via Apple's standard, opt-in crash reporting.

What we never do

  • We never sell data. There is nothing to sell.
  • We never share data with advertisers or data brokers. The app contains zero ad SDKs.
  • We never track you across other apps or websites (no ATT prompt because there is no tracking).
  • We cannot read your ledger, your receipts, or your location history — they never reach us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases every record, receipt, and setting on your device (and your private iCloud copy, when sync is available). There is no copy on our side to delete. Deleting the app deletes its on-device data.

Children

IFTA Tracker is a work tool for commercial drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date. Material changes will be called out in the app.
